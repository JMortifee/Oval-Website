How it works

From first call to final walkthrough

  1. 01

    Survey

    We visit your site, assess access, quantify items and agree a plan that fits your schedule.

  2. 02

    Plan

    You receive a fixed-price quote, a timetable, a labelling system and a risk assessment.

  3. 03

    Move

    Our team packs, disassembles, transports and reinstalls, following the plan step by step.

  4. 04

    Handover

    We check everything is in place, test your IT, collect waste and sign off the completed move.
