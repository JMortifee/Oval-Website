2. The information we collect

We may collect the following information:

5. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it where necessary, with:

  • Our staff, including project managers, fitters and drivers, who need the information to carry out your move.
  • Trusted suppliers, such as subcontracted vehicle hire, licensed waste carriers, secure storage providers and IT specialists, where needed to deliver the services you have booked. They are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Service providers who host our website, process our enquiry form ([name of form provider, e.g. Formspree / Netlify]), and provide our email and accounting tools.
  • Professional advisers, such as accountants, insurers and legal advisers, under duties of confidentiality.
  • Regulators and authorities, where we are required to do so by law.

Some of our providers may process data outside the UK. Where this happens, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or an adequacy regulation.

6. How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a job: deleted or anonymised after 12 months.
  • Customer and job records: kept for 6 years after the end of the job, to meet tax and legal requirements.
  • Marketing contacts: kept until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.
  • Website server logs: kept for up to 90 days.

Archiving and storage services: where you have asked us to store or archive records, we handle any personal data within those records under the terms of your service agreement.

8. Your rights

  • restrict or object to our processing in certain circumstances;
  • request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent;

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@ovalservices.co.uk. We will respond within one month. We may need to verify your identity before we act on your request.
